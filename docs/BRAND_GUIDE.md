# Nexx EventPass — Brand and UX standards

**Tagline:** Events Made Simple. Entry Made Secure.

**Core value:** Organise events, verify payments, issue protected entry passes and record admission.

## Identity
- Primary dark background: `#0B1020`
- Surface: `#171F33`
- Violet: `#8B5CF6`
- Teal: `#45D6C9`
- Success: `#43D7A1`
- Warning: `#FBBF24`
- Rejection: `#FB7185`
- White text: `#F8FAFF`; secondary text: `#A4B1C8`
- Typography: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans; bold headings and accessible body text
- Identity symbol: ticket-inspired N with a scanning-frame corner.

## Components
1. Public marketing: clean branded hero, discovery cards and transparent purchase steps.
2. Guest wallet: event/time/venue and valid server-issued QR, single-entry status.
3. Organiser dashboard: events, sales, pending bank transfer verification, staff access and metrics.
4. Scanner: largest primary action; clear camera permission messaging; strong feedback in text + icon + colour.
5. Platform admin: restricted settings, audit trail and overview only.

## Do not misrepresent security
A branded QR graphic is not a real ticket. Ticket validity requires an authenticated server check and atomic redemption. **Never** embed organiser keywords or signing keys in published images. A sample pass must be clearly identified as illustrative.

## Exports
The downloadable brand kit includes PNG icon sizes 192/512/1024, transparent mark, dark wordmark, social banner, ticket design mockup and concept board. SVG identity mark is version-controlled under `public/brand/`.
